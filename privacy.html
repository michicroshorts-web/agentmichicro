Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 21, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how MichiCroShorts ("the Application") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

2. Data We Collect

MichiCroShorts is a local desktop application. We collect and process the following data:

  • TikTok Authentication Tokens: Stored locally on your device to maintain your session with TikTok's API. These are never transmitted to any server other than TikTok's official API endpoints.
  • Video Files: Your video files are read from your local device and uploaded directly to TikTok. They are not stored or copied by the Application beyond the upload process.
  • Configuration Data: App settings and preferences are stored locally on your device.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

MichiCroShorts does not:

  • Collect personal information such as your name, email, or address
  • Use analytics, tracking, or telemetry
  • Share any data with third parties (other than TikTok's API for uploads)
  • Store your TikTok password
  • Transmit data to any servers owned or operated by us

4. Third-Party Services

The Application communicates with TikTok's API to perform uploads and manage content. Your interactions with TikTok are governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Storage and Security

All data (tokens, cookies, configuration) is stored locally on your device. We recommend keeping your operating system and the Application up to date to maintain security. You can delete all stored data at any time by removing the Application's configuration files.

6. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can:

  • View all stored data in the Application's configuration directory
  • Delete any or all stored data at any time
  • Revoke TikTok API access through your TikTok account settings

7. Children's Privacy

MichiCroShorts is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the Application's support channels.
